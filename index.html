How to Play

ID the ordnance item shown in the image. You have 6 attempts.

The image becomes less blurry after each incorrect guess.

For each attempt, guess the item's category, subcategory, country of origin, and diameter (in mm).

Green means your guess is correct. For diameter, guesses within +/- 7.5% of the real answer count as correct.

Yellow means your guess is close. For country, this means the country is in the same geographic region. For diameter, this means your guess is within 25% of the correct diameter.

Grey means your guess is incorrect.

For diameter, ↑ means guess higher and ↓ means guess lower. Two arrows mean your guess is more than 25% away.
